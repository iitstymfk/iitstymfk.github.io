# Zombies game asset credits
- `scifi/`: Sci-Fi Essentials Kit (Standard) by Quaternius, CC0 (quaternius.com).
- `zombie/skeleton-zombie.glb`: Free Skeleton Zombie by Blaine Ritz / Winged Relay Asset Studio. Used inside this game under its license (no standalone redistribution).
- `guns/pistol.glb`, `guns/revolver.glb`, `guns/shotgun.glb`: from "Pistols & Revolvers - 18 3D Handguns" by Akuyzer (AI-generated with Tripo3D). Free to use in games; the raw files must not be redistributed on their own. Reduced to 30k triangles and 1K textures.
- `guns/ar.glb`, `guns/rifle.glb`: from "All The Assault Rifles (Too Scale)" (itch.io). Materials added.
- `guns/smg.glb` (Kriss Vector), `guns/lmg.glb` (AA-12 drum), `guns/sniper.glb` (AWM): from "Guns_Pack" (itch.io).
- `guns/plasma.glb`: Gun_Pistol from the Quaternius Sci-Fi Essentials Kit (CC0).

## First-person arms
- **PSX First Person Arms** by DrillImpact (free, itch.io: https://drillimpact.itch.io/psx-first-person-arms-free). Rigged arms with knife and hand animations; used for the hands holding every gun and the knife swing.

## Added 2026-11-26 (Tyler's second asset drop)
- **Prisoner zombie** (character + walk/attack/death/dying/idle/hit animations): Mixamo (Adobe), taken from the "Rail Shooter" Unity package. Mixamo characters/animations are free to use in games; not for re-selling as raw files.
- **Sound effects**: Zombie Massacre Sound Effects Pack 01 (zombie attack/pain vocals, gore, head smash, machete, desert eagle, machine gun, crossbow), trimmed and converted to mp3.
- **Textures**: Screaming Brain Studios Tiny Texture Packs (CC0): Tile_02 floor, Wood_17 planks, Metal_22 riveted metal, Bricks_18 stone.
